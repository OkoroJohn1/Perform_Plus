What it actually tracks

Every number already means something to your registrar.

Perform+ doesn't invent new metrics — it computes the ones your department already grades you on, correctly, every time a result changes.

Any grading scale

5.0, 4.0, or your institution's own point system and classification bands — the CGPA engine is built to the scheme, not a generic default.

Carryovers, tracked honestly

Every repeat attempt grouped against the original failing grade — cleared or outstanding, stated plainly, not buried in a spreadsheet column.

The exact average you need

Set a target classification and see the real required average for your remaining semesters — never just a vague "aim higher."

Scan a registration slip

Snap a photo of your course registration and the courses are drafted for you — you fill in the grades once results are out.

A calm, honest advisor

Insights on your goal pace, weakest credit load and trend — grounded in your real record, never a fabricated congratulation.

Your results, on your device

Local-first by design — your record works fully offline, and nothing about your grades leaves your device unless you choose to back it up.
